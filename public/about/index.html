About me

Hi, I'm Peter – I'm originally from the Netherlands, and I've been living in Edinburgh for a few years now (with a decade in Sweden in my late teens and early twenties). I got into photography as a hobby and honestly just enjoy it more and more every time I pick up my camera. I enjoy shooting a bit of everything: street scenes, events, portraits, food. Edinburgh's a great city for it! This website is mostly a place to put my photos and occasionally write something about them. Hope you find something you like. If you'd like to get in touch, see the Contact form.
