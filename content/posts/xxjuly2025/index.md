#### June 2021, Dalby, Sweden

My mum walked into the living room, a few days after _that day_, holding stacks of old photos. You probably know the kind - paper packs filled with photos developed at a local shop. As she sat down and went through them, it was like a small window opened up. A window into a life before me or my brother, when it was just my dad and my mum living life together. I’d heard stories, of course, and we still have some family friends from their time in university, but I hadn’t seen many photos.
